## Goal

Help the operator decide which VB-Audio product, manual, installer, or license information is relevant.

## Use When

- The user asks what to install or buy.
- The user asks whether Matrix, Voicemeeter, VB-CABLE, VBAN, or a manual applies.
- The user needs links or summarized product differences.

## Workflow

1. Research from official VB-Audio pages or the repo docs where possible.
2. Summarize the relevant product, version, manual, and platform constraints.
3. Separate facts from recommendations.
4. Ask before any installer download, installer launch, UAC prompt, payment, donation, or license decision.
5. If the next step is MCP setup, switch to `vbmatrix-setup`.

## Boundaries

- Do not enter payment information.
- Do not accept license terms for the operator.
- Do not run installers or driver changes without explicit approval.
- Do not browse unrelated mirrors when official sources are available.
